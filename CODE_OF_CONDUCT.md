# 行为准则 Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (abridged)

友善待人，表达精确，善意推定。欢迎技术争论；不接受人身攻击、骚扰与垃圾信息。违规的评论、PR 或贡献者，维护者可移除。举报请私下联系维护者（渠道见 `SECURITY.md`）。
Be kind, be precise, assume good faith. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, and spam are not. Maintainers may remove comments, PRs, or contributors that violate this. Reports: contact the maintainer privately (see `SECURITY.md` for the channel).
