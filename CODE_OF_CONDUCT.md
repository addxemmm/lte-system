# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (abridged)

Be kind, be precise, assume good faith. Technical disagreement is welcome;
personal attacks, harassment, and spam are not. Maintainers may remove
comments, PRs, or contributors that violate this. Reports: contact the
maintainer privately (see `SECURITY.md` for the channel).

The full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
